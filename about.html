About me

I am an Assistant Professor at Monash University Indonesia. My research sits at the intersection of medical imaging, machine learning and equitable AI for healthcare, with previous work spanning MRI, image segmentation, quality control, prostate imaging, stroke and tuberculosis.

This website is designed as a concise academic hub for research, people, publications, grants, teaching, talks and media.
